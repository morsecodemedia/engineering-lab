# Engineering Lab

Engineering Lab organizes engineering knowledge into disciplines.

A discipline represents a distinct area of engineering practice.

Capabilities belong to disciplines.

Engineering Judgment influences every discipline.

## Engineering Judgment

> How engineers think.

Cross-cutting. No capabilities. Only principles.

# Systems (formerly Repository)

Purpose:

> Understand existing engineering system.

Questions:

* What is this system?
* How is it organized?
* How does it work?
* Where should I begin?
* What depends on what?
* What risks exists?

Capabilities:

